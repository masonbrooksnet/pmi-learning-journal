# PMI Agile Certified Practitioner (PMI-ACP)® Exam Resources & Blueprint 🏃‍♂️⚡️

This guide outlines practical study strategies, core Agile methodologies (Scrum, Kanban, XP, Lean), and domain breakdowns required to pass the PMI Agile Certified Practitioner (PMI-ACP)® exam and apply adaptive project management in real-world team environments.

---

### 💡 High-Yield Study Strategies & Agile Mindset

* **Embrace Adaptive Over Predictive:** Always prioritize value-driven delivery, customer feedback loops, and iterative refinement over rigid initial plans.
* **Master Framework Trade-offs:** Understand when to apply Scrum (timeboxed sprints), Kanban (continuous flow and WIP limits), or XP (engineering practices like TDD and pair programming).
* **Servant Leadership & Facilitation:** Focus on removing team impediments, empowering cross-functional self-organizing teams, and driving transparent stakeholder communication.
* **Relative Estimation & Velocity:** Get comfortable with Agile sizing metrics (Story Points, Ideal Days, Planning Poker, Planning T-shirt Sizing) and tracking velocity via Burn-down/Burn-up charts.

---

### 🎯 Core Agile Domain Breakdown

* **Domain I. Agile Principles and Mindset (16%):** Agile Manifesto values, self-organizing team culture, continuous improvement, and psychological safety.
* **Domain II. Value-Driven Delivery (20%):** Prioritization techniques (MoSCoW, Kano Model, ROI/NPV/IRR), MVP definition, and frequent increments release planning.
* **Domain III. Stakeholder Engagement (17%):** Wireframes, user story mapping, personas, active listening, and transparent information radiators.
* **Domain IV. Team Performance (16%):** Team formation stages (Tuckman model), servant leadership, cross-functional coaching, and velocity tracking.
* **Domain V. Adaptive Planning (12%):** Progressive elaboration, release/iteration planning, timeboxing, and story point estimations.
* **Domain VI. Problem Detection and Resolution (10%):** Root cause analysis (5 Whys, Fishbone diagram), risk burn-down charts, and retrospective action items.
* **Domain VII. Continuous Improvement (Product, Process, People) (9%):** Retrospectives, process tailing, value stream mapping, and quality metrics.
