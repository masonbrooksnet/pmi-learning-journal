## 💡 A Student's Take on Project Management

When I first started reading project management material, it felt like a maze of heavy terminology and inputs/outputs. But once you start running real team assignments like actual projects, everything clicks. 

### My Core Learnings So Far:
* **Tailoring Over Strict Process**: There is no one-size-fits-all methodology. Predictive (waterfall), adaptive (agile), or hybrid approaches must be tailored to the team's culture and project risk profile.
* **People First, Processes Second**: The shift in the PMP exam towards the *People* domain (42% of the exam!) is real. Leadership, conflict resolution, and stakeholder engagement matter far more than just drawing Gantt charts.
* **Embrace the Hybrid Spectrum**: Most modern software and engineering teams don't run pure waterfall or pure Scrum—they operate in a hybrid reality.

## 🎯 Key Knowledge Areas & Framework Breakdown

### 1. The Three Core Domains (PMP Exam Alignment)
* **People (42%)**: Managing conflict, supporting team performance, empowering team members, and negotiating project agreements.
* **Process (50%)**: Assessing risks, managing communications, executing budget/schedule baselines, and managing quality deliverables.
* **Business Environment (8%)**: Planning organizational compliance, delivering project benefits/value, and evaluating external business changes.

### 2. Traditional (Predictive) vs. Agile (Adaptive)
* **Scope Baseline**: WBS (Work Breakdown Structure) & Scope Statement vs. Product Backlog & User Stories.
* **Change Management**: Formal Change Control Board (CCB) reviews vs. Backlog refinement and Sprint Retrospectives.
* **Delivery Value**: Single final handover vs. Incremental working increments at the end of each iteration.

### 3. Business Analysis & Risk (PMI-PBA / PMI-RMP Insights)
* **Requirements Traceability Matrix (RTM)**: Ensuring every technical task maps back to a valid business need.
* **Risk Response Strategies**: Escalate, Avoid, Transfer, Mitigate, Accept (for threats) vs. Exploit, Share, Enhance, Accept (for opportunities).
