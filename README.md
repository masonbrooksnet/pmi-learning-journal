Welcome to my personal project management learning hub! 

I'm documenting how traditional **PMBOK® Guide** standards and modern **Agile frameworks** apply to real-world scenarios—moving away from pure rote memorization to truly understanding how projects are initiated, planned, executed, and delivered.

---

### 💡 Key Mindset Shifts for Project Managers

* **Tailoring Over Strict Process:** There is no one-size-fits-all methodology. Choose predictive, adaptive, or hybrid delivery models based on project uncertainty and risk.
* **Servant Leadership:** Focus on removing impediments, empowering cross-functional teams, and facilitating stakeholder alignment.
* **Continuous Value Delivery:** Prioritize high-value backlog items early to maximize business outcome and ROI.
