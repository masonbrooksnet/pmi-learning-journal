# PMBOK® Guide & Agile Framework Integration Notes

Modern project management requires tailoring traditional predictive approaches (PMBOK® Guide - 7th Edition) with adaptive Agile/Scrum delivery models to maximize business value and stakeholder engagement.

> **Key Takeaway:** Tailoring project performance domains based on organizational culture, complexity, and uncertainty is critical for successful delivery.

## 1. Predictive vs. Adaptive Delivery Life Cycles

* **Predictive (Waterfall):** Requirements defined upfront, scope strictly managed via change requests, baseline plan driven by critical path method.
* **Adaptive (Agile/Iterative):** Scope refined continuously via Product Backlog refinement, work delivered in short sprints/iterations with continuous customer feedback.
* **Hybrid:** Combining predictive planning for high-risk core modules with agile iterations for customer-facing interfaces.

## 2. PMP ECO (Exam Content Outline) 3 Domains

* **People (42%):** Conflict management, team leadership, empowering team members, stakeholder engagement, and training.
* **Process (50%):** Risk management, scope definition, schedule/budget control, quality management, and procurement execution.
* **Business Environment (8%):** Compliance, organizational value delivery, and supporting organizational transformation.

## 3. Key Agile Frameworks & Ceremonies

* **Scrum Ceremonies:** Sprint Planning, Daily Standup, Sprint Review, Sprint Retrospective.
* **Kanban Principles:** Visualize workflow, limit Work-In-Progress (WIP), manage flow, and make process policies explicit.
